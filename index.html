※ Biography

Builder of small, useful things.

I'm a self-taught engineer who fell sideways into design and never looked back. I work across the stack but I'm happiest where the pixels meet the database — that thin layer where decisions about data become decisions about how a person feels using the thing.

Most of my work lives in the African tech ecosystem: agriculture, logistics, fintech, and the small-business tools that quietly keep markets running. I write code in TypeScript and Go, I sketch in Figma, and I keep a notebook of CSS tricks I'll probably never use.
